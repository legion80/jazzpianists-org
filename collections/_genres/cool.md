Cool jazz favored clarity, restraint, and formal balance, often emphasizing long melodic lines and controlled rhythmic flow. Pianists explored linear counterpoint and harmonic transparency, reducing overt rhythmic emphasis in favor of continuity and precision. The result is a style that foregrounds structure and proportion over expressive immediacy.
