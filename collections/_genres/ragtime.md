Ragtime, along with blues, were the most important antecedents of jazz. While both were developed by black musicians, ragtime was more tied to Western European music, as it was centered around the piano and the music was fully notated.

Closely related to military marches, all rags incorporate several melodies, or *strains*. These strains are usually 16 measures long, and are played twice in sequence. So, for a common rag containing 4 strains, the form would be AABBCCDD.
