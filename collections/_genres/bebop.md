Bebop marked a decisive shift in jazz language, prioritizing rapid tempos, asymmetrical phrasing, and advanced harmonic movement. Piano playing became more linear, with the right hand articulating extended melodic lines while the left hand supplied sparse, rhythmically displaced support. This period represents one of the clearest stylistic ruptures in the history of jazz piano.
